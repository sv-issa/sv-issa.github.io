This talk will discuss privacy fundamentals and then highlight and dig into the ways privacy and information security can help or work against each other; how information security and privacy are different at the enterprise level from the consumer level; why privacy is about more than "confidentiality," and how many security technologies are really PETs (privacy enabling technologies) and are a person's best friend, but can go feral if implemented incorrectly.
