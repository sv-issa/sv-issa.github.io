**Zee.Aero**
Mountain View, CA, United States
Full-Time

Zee.Aero is developing revolutionary aircraft concepts, working at the intersection of aerodynamics, autonomy, and electric propulsion. We are actively building a team of world-class engineers who have experience in fields ranging from power electronics to aerodynamics.

We are currently seeking an IT Support Specialist with substantial desktop support experience spanning Mac OS X, Linux, and Windows. The successful applicant will be responsible for cross-platform user support, some network and server administration, and systems automation. Solid IT support and administration experience is essential for the candidate to join a small IT team that holds agility and efficiency in high regard, runs highly virtualized infrastructure, admins a supercomputer, and keeps things humming.

**Requirements**

- Bachelors Degree in Computer Science, or equivalent
- Minimum three years experience providing technical support in a multi-platform user environment
- A positive user-facing attitude that mixes customer service with user education and empowerment
- Ability to move quickly in a fast-paced startup setting
- Strong capabilities in Mac OS X, Linux, and Windows
- Solid understanding of TCP/IP network fundamentals
- Experience with network-based modular deployment
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Strong critical thinking skills

**Desired Skills**

- Experience with version control (mainly git)
- Shell scripting capabilities
- Apache administration experience
- Automation experience using munki, puppet, and Active Directory
- Experience supporting CAD tools and workflows
- Ability to exercise good judgement, independently assess risk, and make critical decisions
- A healthy sense of adventure

Applicants are strongly encouraged to include a cover letter when applying.

We are located in Mountain View just a couple of hundred feet from Shoreline Lake and its surrounding walking/jogging trails. We offer a comprehensive benefits package including medical, dental, vision and life insurance, 20 PTO days, 10 company holidays, a 401(k) plan with company matching, commuter checks, and education assistance. And on top of all that, we also offer a variety of perks such as free lunches, snacks, drinks, fruits, year-end bonuses, employee referral bonus, IP bonus program, ping-pong table, afternoon chocolate, happy hours and many more.
