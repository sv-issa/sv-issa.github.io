The Holiday Season is here. To begin the Season, in traditional style, we invite you to join the Silicon Valley Chapter ISSA for our Annual Holiday Gathering. Bring your colleagues and friends.

Our holiday event will be on the evening of Tuesday, December 18th, 4pm to 6pm in Sunnyvale. This year we're doing an evening cocktail and appetizer event.

The fare: Members $25 and Non Members $35. We are only accepting payments in advance either via check or Paypal of $25 ISSA members and $35 for non-members. RSVP Must be received by Wednesday, December 12th.

Have a safe and joyful holiday season!
