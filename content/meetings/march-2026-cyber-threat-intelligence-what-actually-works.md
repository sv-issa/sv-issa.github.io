Threat intelligence is everywhere. Feeds, dashboards, reports, alerts. Yet many organizations still struggle to turn intelligence into meaningful action.

Join Silicon Valley ISSA for a candid fireside conversation with Cyber Threat Intelligence Expert Joe Slowik and Chapter President Mike Skurko as they cut through the noise and explore what actually works in modern threat intelligence.

This discussion will move beyond theory and marketing language. Expect practical insights drawn from operational experience across government, national labs, and the private sector.

---

## Key Topics Covered

• What separates useful intelligence from noise
• How to operationalize threat intelligence inside real security programs
• Lessons from incident response that change how you approach CTI
• Government and private sector intelligence differences
• Alerting strategy and signal quality in modern environments
• Common mistakes organizations make with threat feeds and reporting
• How security leaders should evaluate intelligence investments
