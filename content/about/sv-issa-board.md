The board of directors for the Silicon Valley Chapter of the Information Systems Security Association (ISSA)  is comprised of individuals who have not only expressed interest in contributing to the greater good of the Information Security community and the community-at-large, but have also volunteered their time to take action towards achieving this goal. With representation from some of the largest Silicon Valley companies to niche boutiques, we work hard to facilitate fulfillment of our members' objectives.

Feel free to contact the appropriate person from the list below.

 

Mike Skurko

President: [president@sv-issa.org](mailto:president@sv-issa.org)

Mike Skurko is the President of the Silicon Valley ISSA. He is the Co-Founder and Principal Consultant at [PRE](https://www.preglobalsolutions.com/) working exclusively with cybersecurity startups as a vCRO and Advisor providing security strategies, GTM, and revenue generation. He's worked for or advised over 23 startups and seen two IPOs and four acquisitions. Beyond his day job(s) in cybersecurity he holds three non-cyber security certifications: [Pyrotechnic Operator](https://pyroinnovations.com/pyrotechnics_license_requirements.html), [Avy-1](https://avtraining.org/aiare-level-1/) and is a [PADI Rescue Diver](https://www.padi.com/courses/rescue-diver). He was raised in Tokyo, speaks the Japanese language and is well versed in the culture *and* cuisine of Japan.

Volunteer Director: [volunteer@sv-issa.org](mailto:volunteer@sv-issa.org)

He has held leadership positions at Amdocs and Meta (Facebook), including the CISO role at Vubiquity, media subsidiary of Amdocs, working with major Hollywood Studios and major streaming providers. In addition to his role at Xage, Victor is advising multiple early-stage startups in cybersecurity, AI infrastructure, and DevOps automation. He has partnerships with multiple Silicon Valley Venture Funds to advise portfolio companies and work closely with Plug and Play Tech Center, a startup accelerator headquartered in Sunnyvale, California. Victor volunteers his time at the nonprofit Pocket Security as Director of Security Assessments to help secure other nonprofits in need of raising their cybersecurity maturity. This volunteer role enables Victor to further apply his passion for cybersecurity to nonprofits in need of security assessment, cybersecurity awareness training, and vCISO advisory services.

Kelly Robertson

Education Director: [educationdirector@sv-issa.org](mailto:educationdirector@sv-issa.org)

Kelly is a security practitioner with more than 30 years of experience protecting organizations across industries and continents. As Principal Security Practitioner at Zisher InfoSec, he brings together deep technical expertise and executive-level perspective — the product of a career that spans the U.S. Air Force, SAIC, Nokia, NetScreen, Juniper Networks, and Horizon3.ai, along with a decade leading information security consulting firms as CEO. His practice covers the full spectrum of security work: infrastructure and application assessments, secure software development lifecycle, information security management systems, governance and regulatory compliance, policy and process development, security operations, and vCISO engagements. He doesn't teach theory — he teaches what actually happens when security meets the real world. Kelly serves as Education Director for the ISSA Silicon Valley Chapter, where he develops and delivers programs designed to build practitioners, not test-takers. The G.R.I.T. program is the fullest expression of that conviction — a cohort-based experience built on the belief that the security community needs professionals who do the work.
