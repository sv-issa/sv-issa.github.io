October 2022 SV-ISSA **hybrid in-person** event-

While AI is transforming what systems can do, it requires real data to learn from. Whenever you ask an AI engineer how much data they need, the answer is always "all of it". While this is the right answer for the researcher, it isn't always the right answer for our customers.

In this talk we will discuss real world the nature of the data risk organizations face and how confidential compute can secure AI training.
