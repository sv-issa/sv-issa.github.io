It is time to elect a few positions for the Silicon Valley Chapter ISSA board of directors.

Open positions in this cycle are:

* Events Director
* Secretary
* Education Director
* Webmaster
* President (prior board service requirements apply)

Send your nominations to [elections@sv-issa.org](mailto:elections@sv-issa.org).

Must be a member in good standing to apply.
