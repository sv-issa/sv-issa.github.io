AppDynamics is looking for a Field Security Architect to support its business opportunities throughout North America and Europe. This position reports into the Product Security Assurance Team in the CISO Org of AppDynamics.

This is not a sales role but rather works closely with sales, pre-sales engineering, post sales services, technical operations, and product management counterparts to better understand customer security concerns around utilizing cloud services including AppDynamics' SaaS offerings. You will be able to establish themselves as a trusted advisor to our customers and develop credibility as a security industry authority focusing on our customers' security and privacy priorities as it relates to the usage of the suite of AppDynamics products
