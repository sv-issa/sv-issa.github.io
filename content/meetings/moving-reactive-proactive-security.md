Much of what security experts today spend their time doing is reacting to threats. Tools available are very much focused tracking user behavior, evaluating destination trust and documenting malicious results. The email industry has been no different, until now. With the inception of Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance (DMARC), organizations can realistically protect their brand, restore trust in their domains and virtually eliminate unwanted email worldwide.
This discussion is intended to provoke thought in how to move to a proactive stance, focus on email borne threats and educate on DMARC and being proactive for at least the email portion of the information security spectrum
