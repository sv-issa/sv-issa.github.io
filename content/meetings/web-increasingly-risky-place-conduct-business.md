The web is an increasingly risky place to conduct business. The risk is mostly talked about in terms of breaches and the nuances of infinite strains of malware and how to detect them. In this talk, Kowsik will provide detailed insights into the state of the web, the source of risks and why detection technologies are continuing to fail us.
