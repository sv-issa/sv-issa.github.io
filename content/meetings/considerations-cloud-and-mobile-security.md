In many organizations, both employees and line of business managers are pushing for adoption of cloud apps and BYOD, and the IT security team is being forced to comply. But cloud applications store sensitive corporate data outside of the enterprise, raising security and compliance risks. And BYOD means sensitive corporate data is stored on personal mobile devices that may be lost or stolen. This session will cover:

- Areas where cloud app providers are investing in security, and where they are not.
- Where your existing security solutions solve portions of this problem, and what gaps remain.
- Why the employee experience should really matter to you.
- Security coverage to consider when enabling cloud and mobile.
