There is a perception that if an enterprise adopts a cloud service (Salesforce, Google Apps, Office 365, Box), the provider will take care of security. However, under the shared responsibility model, enterprises are actually responsible for access to and usage of corporate data in the cloud. In this session, we will explore real-world customer data on cloud usage, risks and new cloud threats. We will explore the emergence of cloud access security brokers, and the variety of controls and considerations related to cloud security.
