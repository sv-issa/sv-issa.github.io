RSA will present Advanced Incident Response. The presentation will focus on the process, how that ties the many Security Technologies (Controls) together and how that allows us to drastically cut down on the investigative time, critical in an incident. RSA will review their organization pre-breach and post-breach to show how they applied their lessons learned. They will wrap up with a technical demo on host forensics inspection and memory analysis.
