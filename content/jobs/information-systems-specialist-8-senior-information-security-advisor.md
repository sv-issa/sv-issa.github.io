**Duties & Responsibilities**
This position is part of the Information Security & Privacy Office and works under the direction of the Information Security Officer. The Senior Information Security Advisor provides information security expertise throughout the systems development lifecycle in areas including, but not limited to, software development, access and control methodologies, operations, and continuity planning. This position is integral in ensuring the confidentiality, integrity and availability of DHS/OHA system and information resources.

**MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:**
Six years of professional consultative, technical, or administrative experience, providing expert advice and leadership in the planning, development, coordination, and implementation of information systems. Experience must include activities in:

- **Communications:** Communication means the connections that link systems and includes voice, data, image, and video.
- **Software:** Software means the programs, procedures, rules, and associated documentation concerned with operating a system and covers both applications and operating software.
- **Hardware:** Hardware Device means the physical components (e.g., PC, server, mainframe, peripherals).
- **Data:** Data means data bases and associated master files;

**AND EITHER**

(a) at least 30 quarter (20 semester) credits in computer science; **OR**
(b) two more years of experience providing a knowledge of information systems theory and principles;

15 quarter (10 semester) graduate-level computer science, software engineering, information systems, or equivalent credits may substitute for one year of the professional experience.

Note: Transcripts are required to be attached to your application if you are using education or coursework to meet the minimum qualifications. Transcripts must be from an accredited institution and clearly show 1) your name; 2) the name and address of the institution; 3) the degree received and; 4) required courses completed with a passing grade. For application purposes, photocopies are acceptable; however official or original documents may be requested to validate education.

**DESIRED ATTRIBUTES:**
- Experience with implementing and/or ensuring compliance with the NIST 800-53 Rev 4 framework.
- Hands-on experience working in a federally regulated environment and/or with protected data (PII, ePHI, FTI, etc.).
- Familiarity with information security risk assessments and audits.
- Information security experience regarding the physical, technical, and personnel aspects of ensuring the confidentiality, integrity and availability of protected data.
- Current Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) or qualified to complete state-sponsored certification within 12 months of hire.
- Significant expertise in systems and information security.
- Mastered in concepts, methodologies, techniques and tools of systems development security.
- Ability to work in a system development environment that has a mix of web-based technology, database management systems and mainframe interfaces, remote input and manipulation from multiple remote locations and through the web, and a significant amount of expansion or change to existing business processes.
- Demonstrated technical security expertise.
- Experience recommending allocation of funds for technical resources and security hardware/software needs.
- Experience in promoting a culturally competent and diverse work environment.

Applicants who meet the minimum qualifications and most closely match the desired attributes will be invited for an interview.
