Autodesk has four new security engineering openings in San Francisco, CA. Details for each role are below.

[Security Engineer Identity and Data Security](https://autodesk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Ext/job/San-Francisco-CA-USA/Security-Engineer--Identity-and-Data-Security_20WD39060-2)

Your mission will be to ensure that Autodesk and its customers most precious asset, their intellectual property and personal data remains secure. This is an exciting opportunity in a growing infrastructure security team where you will get a chance to work with many different product and security teams on topics ranging from application security, security architecture and engineering, security operations and incident response. You will also get an opportunity to share your ideas, thoughts and problems with your peers outside the company. In this role, you will also have the ability to redefine data security practices in the organization to better fit the changing infrastructure and business landscape. The individual in this role is expected to be an effective communicator and should be able to articulate complex technical topics to technical and business audience. The incumbent should also have a proven ability to work in teams and influence and drive strategy and execution by getting buy in and consensus from the stakeholders involved.

- Plans, designs, develops, operates and consults on solutions to improve the data security posture of products and business systems across the enterprise
- Results achieved through contributions and enhancements to both existing and new processes, tools, and designs
- Strengthens the security of the business through continuous improvement
- Collaborates with team members, members of other teams and customers in producing high-quality deliverables in an efficient manner

[Network Security Engineer](https://autodesk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Ext/job/San-Francisco-CA-USA/Network-Security-Engineer_21WD46301-1)

Autodesk must secure its information assets, services, and products to build stakeholder trust and protect the privacy of our customers. A Senior/Lead Network Security engineer will, design, build, optimize and deploy systems to protect Autodesk and its customers against all types of threats and threat adversaries. This role is seen as an expert on information and security systems. The role will build, secure and operate systems that stop and/or prevent malicious activities, security violations, vulnerabilities and other threats against Autodesk's information assets. This position will understand and research the latest network security threats, vulnerabilities & methods for deploying controls in the prevention, detection and response to best secure the environment. Additionally, the role will evangelize security practices to protect Autodesk against information security risks.

This position will have detailed knowledge of the architecture and behavior of the applications and systems which handle this data. This role will work across many security teams including infrastructure, engineering, operations, product development, incident response, and red teaming. In addition, this position will actively collaborate and share ideas, thoughts and challenges with peers both inside and outside the company.

[Senior Azure Security Engineer](https://autodesk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Ext/job/San-Francisco-CA-USA/Senior-Azure-Security-Engineer_21WD46850-1)

Autodesk Security is seeking a Senior Azure Security Engineer to help design, review, and implement critical security solutions at scale for our Azure public cloud infrastructure. This position is a technical, influential role that will appeal to a candidate with a bias to action and an affinity for learning new technologies. The ideal candidate is autonomous but collaborative and has deep expertise in Azure and one or more security domains and enjoys solving problems in areas outside of their comfort zone.
