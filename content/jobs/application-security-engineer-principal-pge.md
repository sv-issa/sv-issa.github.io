This is a technical leadership position with direct reports and is third party facing. Technical responsibilities include vulnerability research and individual assessments of everything from SCADA systems to mobile applications.

**Requirements**

Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelors Degree in Computer Science or equivalent work experience
- Minimum 8 years of experience performing security assessments or vulnerability research

Desired:

- Must be able to perform network protocol analysis on possibly unknown protocols
- Understands the theory of programming languages enough to be comfortable performing code reviews for possibly unfamiliar languages
- Penetration testing, application security, security researcher
- At least 3 years of consulting experience
- Team leadership experience
- Metasploit, Burp, FreeBSD, Unix, Linux, Web Application Penetration experience

Responsibilities

- Perform application penetration testing in Enterprise environments.
- Identify vulnerabilities and recommend solutions.
- Executes and supports assessments, audits, tests, and verification activities for the service areas.
- Oversees testing of controls for compliance to standards/regulation and governance processes as it relates to the line of business.
- Working knowledge of security domains, auditing standards and frameworks, and risk analysis frameworks.
