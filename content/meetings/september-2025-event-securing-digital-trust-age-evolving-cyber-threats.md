As digital transformation accelerates, businesses are increasingly dependent on secure technologies, data integrity, and trust across their ecosystems. In this session, **Bindi Davé** will share strategies for building and sustaining digital trust amidst evolving cyber threats. Drawing on her leadership experience at GoDaddy, DigiCert, and other global enterprises, she will explore how cybersecurity can drive business growth while protecting critical assets.

Attendees will leave with actionable strategies to strengthen cybersecurity posture, enhance stakeholder trust, and position their organizations for long-term success in an interconnected world.

**Key Topics Covered**

- The Role of Digital Trust in Business Resilience
- Proactive Security Frameworks: IAM, data protection, continuous monitoring
- AI Governance & Responsible AI Security Practices
- Real-World Case Studies & Lessons Learned

**Why Attend**

- Gain insight from a globally recognized cybersecurity leader
- Learn practical, scalable security strategies you can implement now
- Understand how AI governance intersects with trust and resilience
- Network with peers and industry experts in a collaborative setting
