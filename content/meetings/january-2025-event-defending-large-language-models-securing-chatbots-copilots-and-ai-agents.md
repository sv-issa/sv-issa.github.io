Dive into the rapidly evolving world of AI security in this compelling session, *Defending Large Language Models: Securing Chatbots, Copilots, and AI Agents.* Discover the critical and vulnerable points within large language models (LLMs) that power today’s intelligent systems and gain actionable strategies to defend against emerging threats.

Guided by Steve Wilson, a leader in generative AI security and author of the Amazon Best Seller *The Developer’s Playbook for Large Language Model Security*, this session will provide a comprehensive approach to securing LLM systems. Explore the Responsible AI Software Engineering (RAISE) framework, real-world case studies, and best practices tailored for AI-driven software.

This is an essential event for security professionals, AI developers, and leaders navigating the complexities of safeguarding the next generation of intelligent systems.

---

**Key Topics Covered Will Include:**

- Understanding trust boundaries in chatbots, copilots, and AI agents.
- Emerging threats targeting large language models and their defenses.
- A deep dive into the Responsible AI Software Engineering (RAISE) framework.
- Real-world case studies on securing LLM systems.
- Tools, processes, and best practices for securing AI-driven applications.
