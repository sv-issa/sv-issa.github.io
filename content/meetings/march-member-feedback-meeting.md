In March we will be taking a break from usual speaker presentations. During this session we will engage in a conversation with members on how they would like the chapter meetings to be structured going forward.  Potential areas of discussion could include:

- Desired topics or areas for speaker presentations
- Format of chapter meetings and speaker presentations
- Alternative activities for meetings such as socials, trainings, etc

The open forum will be followed by a closed members-only report from the Audit Committee.

Please be join us with your ideas and comments.
