Join us for our June chapter meeting featuring Brian Laing, a renowned cybersecurity expert, who will provide critical insights into navigating the new cybersecurity landscape in 2024. This event promises to be an eye-opening session on the emerging threats and strategies for the current year.

In this talk, you'll gain valuable insights into the rise of Infostealers and their profound impact on businesses, the sophisticated tactics of Dark Web-driven augmented social engineering, and the alarming trend of malicious due diligence targeting C-Suite executives. Learn strategies to fortify your organization's defenses against these evolving threats from industry leader Brian Laing. Equip yourself with the knowledge and tools to stay ahead in the ever-changing cybersecurity landscape.

**Key topics covered will include:**

- The critical cybersecurity threats shaping 2024.
- Strategies to fortify your organization's defenses against emerging threats.
- Practical insights and techniques to navigate the new cybersecurity landscape.

This session will arm you with the knowledge and strategies needed to strengthen your organization's defenses against cyber threats and effectively manage risk.

**Why Attend:**

- Gain insights from a leading expert in the cybersecurity field.
- Learn practical strategies to navigate the new cybersecurity landscape.
- Network with fellow cybersecurity professionals and industry leaders.
