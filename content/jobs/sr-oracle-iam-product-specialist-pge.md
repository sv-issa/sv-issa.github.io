Interested in a career with a company that operates critical infrastructure to deliver energy to 1 in 20 Americans with a geographic span of 70,000 square miles in California? If so, then take a look at the various opportunities at Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E).

Here at PG&E, we're looking for a Sr Oracle IAM Product Specialist with proven operational experience in managing Identity Management solutions. This is a "hands on" operational role primarily responsible for the deployment, configuration and ongoing administration of Identity management products on an enterprise scale. The appointee will have expertise in at least TWO of the following technologies: Oracle Identity Manager, Oracle Directory Server Enterprise Edition, Oracle Virtual Directory, Oracle Identity Analytics, LDAP directory services, CA SiteMinder. This position is a full-time role located in Downtown San Francisco.
