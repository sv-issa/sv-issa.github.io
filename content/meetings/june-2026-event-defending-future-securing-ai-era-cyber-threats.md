Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming cybersecurity—but it’s also reshaping the threat landscape. As adversaries increasingly exploit AI models for offensive cyber operations, organizations must rethink how they secure systems, protect infrastructure, and deploy AI responsibly.

Join us for an exclusive session featuring **Tiffany Saadé**, a leader at the intersection of AI, cybersecurity, and policy. This talk will explore how AI is being misused, how defenders can stay ahead, and what it takes to build secure, resilient AI systems in today’s high-stakes environment.

**Key Topics Covered**

- How adversaries exploit AI models for cyber operations
- AI red-teaming: identifying vulnerabilities before attackers do
- Building secure-by-design AI systems
- Protecting critical infrastructure with AI-driven defenses
- Bridging AI security with policy, governance, and national security
- Real-world applications of AI in cyber defense

- Learn cutting-edge strategies used by industry and government leaders
