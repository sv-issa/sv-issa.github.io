The AppDynamics Information Security Assurance team is seeking a strong team player who has the experience and drive to contribute to a talented team focused on handling customer security engagements. As a member of this team, you will be responsible for streamlining and improving our responses to customer security questions, facilitating product security improvements, and building customer confidence. This role will primarily support the sales and field personnel with security, regulatory, and compliance needs, with some direct external customer interaction as needed.

This senior position will have a high degree of visibility and autonomy to build and improve programs, drive process efficiency, and directly lead projects. Communication and attention to detail are critical to be successful in this role.
