You need an increased budget? -- No way!!! How many times have you been rejected when asking for a budget increase. It seems that security budgets are decreasing in some organizations while peers at other organizations are getting millions. During this one hour session we will explore the art of selling security to peers and executive management. Topics will be broken into three sections:

1. **Persuading & Influencing Peers** - Learn how to work with your peers at your organization to get support for your security initiatives. Together we will explore the ability to get support for security initiatives by just presenting your ideas in the best manner possible. How do we get our peers to go ask for the funds in a team manner?

2. **Methods for Acquiring Budget Increases** - By combining proven sales techniques with our security experience can lead to positive results. Your ability to accurately assess each situation can lead to major budget increases. Come learn valuable techniques used by CISOs to acquire enhanced budgets while making a valuable security contribution to the business.

3. **Pulling Budget from Thin-air** - When we can't get additional budget, how do we review our current security expenditures and pull budget from thin-air without affecting our current security posture? How can we get more from our current budgets without compromising our security?

Attending this session with help you understand what you can do to help prevent your company from being easily attacked and compromised.
