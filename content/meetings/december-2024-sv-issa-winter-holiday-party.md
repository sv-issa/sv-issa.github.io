**Celebrate the Season with Your Peers!**

In warm appreciation of our members, the Silicon Valley ISSA Chapter invites you to our annual Winter Holiday Party.

This is a **member-preferred event**, meaning SV-ISSA Members are given priority registration and exclusive access to this special gathering. Non-members are welcome to register but will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, with no guaranteed entry.

This is your chance to connect with fellow cybersecurity professionals, share stories, and toast to another year of innovation and collaboration. Forget the formalities—there’s no agenda, just an evening of good food, great drinks, and even better company.

Join us for an evening of networking and holiday cheer. We’ll kick things off with drinks and light networking from **5:00 PM to 7:00 PM**, followed by a festive spread of food, drinks, and more networking from **7:00 PM to 9:00 PM**. This event is all about celebrating our vibrant community and the connections that make it thrive.

**Why Attend?**

- **Reconnect and Network:** Engage with peers in a relaxed, festive atmosphere.
- **Celebrate the Year:** Reflect on 2024’s successes and build excitement for 2025.
- **Enjoy the Season:** Savor delicious food and drinks in the company of industry friends.
- **Strengthen Your Community:** Support the Silicon Valley ISSA Chapter by celebrating with us.

**Key Details:**

- **Agenda:**

- 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM: Drinks and Networking
- 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM: Food, Drinks, and More Networking
