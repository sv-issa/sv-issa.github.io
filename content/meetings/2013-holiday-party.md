**GOOD COMPANY OF FRIENDS, COLLEAGUES, DELICIOUS FOOD, & HOLIDAY CHEER!!!!**

The Holiday Season is here. To begin the Season, in traditional style, we invite you to join the Silicon Valley Chapter ISSA for our Annual Holiday Gathering. Bring your colleagues and friends.

Our holiday event will be on Tuesday, December 3rd, 11am to 1:30pm in San Jose.

The fare: Members $25 and Non Members $35.

We are only accepting payments in advance either via check or Paypal of $25 ISSA members and $35 for non-members. If you choose to pay by check please bring it to the November meeting. Please indicate if you would like a vegetarian entree.

Have a safe and joyful holiday season!
