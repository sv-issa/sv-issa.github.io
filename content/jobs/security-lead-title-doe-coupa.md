This position can work out of our San Mateo office.

Do you want to work for a company that The Wall Street Journal named one of the top 50 new start-up companies or the Business Insider states is one of the 25 Enterprise Startups To Bet Your Career on? Coupa is building a world-class company of experienced professionals and we could be looking for you.

Coupa is emerging as one of the hottest Cloud Applications companies. Already managing $10+ billion in spending for worldwide enterprises, Coupa is recognized as the next generation leader of Spend Management solutions. With Coupa, the global enterprise is able to manage expenses, encourages smarter spending with innovative tools, provide real time views, and makes recommendations for organizational improvement.

We are looking for a Security Lead to lead security for the Coupa Cloud platform. This role will involve developing the building blocks to secure the Coupa Cloud for our growing customer base and provide security in compliance with global laws and regulations. This is a hands-on SecDevOps role.

We need people who are self-motivated, have a strong desire to learn, a can-do attitude, tenacity to solve problems, team players, and results focused. We have an unwavering zeal to make our Customers Successful.

**Responsibilities include:**

- Advise product and operations teams on product and infrastructure security as the Security subject matter expert (SME).
- Perform vulnerability assessments and resolve security issues from assessments and other sources.
- Investigate security incidents in coordination with internal teams and customer security teams.
- Lead the 24x7 Security Operations (SOC) team.
- Define and operationalize security standards, policies, and procedures.
- Architect, deploy and maintain security tools including intrusion detection systems, firewalls, anti-virus, log analysis, static code analysis, application security, SSO, SIEM and custom tools.

**Requirements:**

- Bachelor's Degree.
- 3+ years of experience in an equivalent security related role.
- Familiarity with Cloud Computing and Software as a Service.
- Hands-on operational experience with security tools such as intrusion detection systems, firewalls, anti-virus, log analysis, static code analysis, application security, SSO and SIEM.
- 2+ years of experience with programming using Java, Perl, PHP, Python, Ruby or equivalent.
- Strong communications skills, both written and oral.
- On hire, must take and pass a Moderate Public Trust background investigation or within the last 2 years has taken and passed a Moderate Public Trust background investigation or higher.

Coupa offers a very comprehensive and competitive benefit package along with a creative and exciting place to work. If interested, please send your resume to careers@coupa.com. Please be advised, inquiries or resumes from recruiters will NOT be accepted.
