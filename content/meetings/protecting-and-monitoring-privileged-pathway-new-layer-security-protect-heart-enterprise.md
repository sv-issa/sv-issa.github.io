When speaking on the recent attacks, the FBI's cyber division stated that "the malwares that are used would have probably gotten past about 90 percent of the defenses that are out there today both in industry and in government." This highlights the insufficiency of perimeter defenses, and the need for an internal layer of security around privileged accounts.

In this session, Barak Feldman will discuss best practices that focus on proactively protecting and monitoring all privileged account activity inside the network, covering all IT assets within the data center. We will discuss this new dimension modern attackers are focusing on – the keys to the kingdom and the most power they can achieve within an organization infrastructure.
