**CISSP Certification Preparation Course starting August 17**

Gain the skills and knowledge needed to pass the CISSP exam in this intensive 10-week evening course offered by Silicon Valley ISSA.

Over the 10 sessions, our experienced coaches will guide you through all 8 CISSP domains, integrating:

- Real-world examples and scenario activities
- Group discussions on critical topics
- Knowledge checks and chapter quizzes
- Exam practice tests and tips

Everything is based on the latest CISSP exam outline and materials.

Our virtual class environment ensures focused attention.

### Who Should Attend:

Infosec professionals looking to get certified.

Must have at least 5 years experience for CISSP, which may be waived for an Associate CISSP certification.

### Benefits:

- Vastly improve your chances of passing the CISSP exam
- Network with local cybersecurity experts
- Up to 5 scholarships available for students
- Earn up to 80 CPE credits

### When:

Tuesdays from 6pm to 9pm starting August 17.

Runs for 10 consecutive weeks.

### Where:

ISSA Silicon Valley Chapter
