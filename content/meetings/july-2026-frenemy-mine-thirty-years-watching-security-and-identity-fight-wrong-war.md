Sixty-plus years of authentication history, and the login box is still the thing that makes grown security engineers visibly wince. This talk traces the arc from the invention of the password in 1961 through hardware tokens, SMS OTP, push authentication, and MFA fatigue, to the passkey era — *finally* an interesting solution, with a dispiriting adoption curve. I'll use the Four Ps framework from my book *Mastering Digital Identity* to name the design flaw running through all six decades — and for those of you experiencing authentication depression, there's a prescription at the end.

**Key Topics Covered**

- Why login experiences still frustrate users and security teams
- The evolution of MFA: hardware tokens, SMS OTP, push, and beyond
- MFA fatigue attacks and modern authentication threats
- Passkeys: promise, reality, and adoption challenges
- The “Four Ps” framework from *Mastering Digital Identity*
- Designing authentication experiences people can actually use
- What the future of identity and trust looks like
