The following security education programs are being offered at universities and colleges in Northern California. The listing of these programs does not imply endorsement by Silicon Valley ISSA. This is only a first cut at creating this list. If members are aware of additional security programs in the Bay Area, please send an email to the Education Director at [educationdirector@sv-issa.org](mailto:educationdirector@sv-issa.org).

  [seclab.cs.ucdavis.edu](http://seclab.cs.ucdavis.edu/)

### Sacramento State University
- Center for Information Assurance and Security
  [www.ecs.csus.edu/csc/iac/](http://www.ecs.csus.edu/csc/iac/)

### Cal Poly Pomona
- Center for Information Assurance

The Center for Information Assurance (CIA) at the Cal Poly Pomona College of Business Administration (CBA) provides advanced research and knowledge in audit, security and computer forensics.

[www.bus.csupomona.edu/cfia.asp](http://www.bus.csupomona.edu/cfia.asp)

### Naval Postgraduate School
- Center for Information Systems Security Studies and Research

CISR is America's foremost center for defense-related research in Information Assurance (IA), Inherently Trustworthy Systems (ITC), and defensive information warfare.

[cisr.nps.edu](http://cisr.nps.edu/)
