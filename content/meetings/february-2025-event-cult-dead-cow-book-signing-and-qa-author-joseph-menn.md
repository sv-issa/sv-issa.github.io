Join us for an exclusive event hosted by the Silicon Valley ISSA! Chapter President Mike Skurko will lead an engaging Q&A and discussion with acclaimed cybersecurity journalist Joseph Menn, author of the bestseller *Cult of the Dead Cow: How the Original Hacking Supergroup Might Just Save the World.* Dive into the origins of hacktivism and ethical security work, and gain insights from Menn's extensive career in cybersecurity journalism. After the discussion, enjoy a book signing where all in-person attendees will receive a free copy of the book!

**Key Topics Covered:**

- The rise of hacktivism and ethical hacking
- Behind-the-scenes stories from *Cult of the Dead Cow*
- Insights into modern cybersecurity threats
- Ethical security practices and industry challenges
- Open Q&A with a leading cybersecurity journalist

**Why Attend:**

- Exclusive access to cybersecurity thought leaders
- Free signed copy of *Cult of the Dead Cow* for in-person attendees
- Network with cybersecurity professionals and enthusiasts
- Gain valuable insights into the future of ethical hacking
