The role of the CISO has evolved far beyond technical controls and compliance checklists — today’s security leaders must inspire trust, enable business growth, and shape organizational culture in an increasingly complex threat landscape.

Join Silicon Valley ISSA for an engaging panel discussion moderated by Edward Frye, Head of Security at Luminary Cloud and past President of the Silicon Valley ISSA Chapter, featuring two renowned security leaders:

- Jessica Hoffman, Deputy CISO for the City of Philadelphia and cybersecurity educator
- Larry Whiteside Jr., veteran CISO, co-founder of Cyversity, and longtime advocate for diversity and leadership in cybersecurity

Together, they’ll share real-world lessons from leading security programs across public and private sectors — from building trust with executives and aligning security with business goals, to navigating compliance frameworks, mentoring the next generation of talent, and partnering effectively with vendors.

Whether you’re a practitioner looking to grow into leadership, a CISO navigating modern challenges, or part of a sales or marketing team that supports the cybersecurity ecosystem, this discussion will deliver actionable insights, candid stories, and inspiration for the year ahead.

Key Takeaways:

- Practical strategies for aligning security with business and mission outcomes
- Insights into public vs. private sector security leadership
- Lessons in mentorship, inclusion, and career growth
- How to build meaningful partnerships between CISOs, vendors, and executives
