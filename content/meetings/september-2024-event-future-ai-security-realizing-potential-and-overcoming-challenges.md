AI is often touted as the future of cybersecurity, but how far have we really come? Join us for an insightful panel discussion featuring industry leaders at the forefront of integrating AI into security practices. This event will explore the current state of AI in security, the practical challenges of implementation, and the future outlook. Whether you're curious about AI's potential or looking for strategies to enhance your security operations, this panel will provide valuable takeaways. The event will conclude with a special Q&A session with ISSA International President Jimmy Sanders, offering attendees the opportunity to engage directly with the speakers.

**Key Topics Covered Will Include:**

- The real impact of AI on security workflows today
- Challenges in scaling AI solutions for security
- How automation and AI are transforming security practices
- The future of AI in security: trends to watch

**Why Attend:**

- **Expert Insights:** Hear from pioneers in AI and cybersecurity who are shaping the future of the industry.
- **Practical Applications:** Learn how to effectively leverage AI to enhance your security posture, automate workflows, and manage emerging threats.

- **Networking Opportunities:** Connect with fellow professionals and industry leaders in the cybersecurity community.
