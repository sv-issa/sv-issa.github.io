**Description:**

Are you a multi-tasking, PCI compliance pro with experience training and consulting on PCI validation? Then PayPros is looking for you! PayPros is looking for a Compliance Services Specialist to support our partners and merchants to meet and sustain PCI validation requirements. The successful candidate will have working knowledge of PCI Security Standards and the payments industry. They must also have demonstrated ability to implement, support and communicate work efforts with the director and other internal and external customers. If this sounds like you or someone you know please feel free to review the qualifications below:

**Qualifications:**

**Knowledge, Skills and Abilities:**

- Ability to assist our client partners through the PayPros PCI validation process.
- Ability to manage and multi-task through a large support case load.
- Ability to train and support others on the PayPros Team.

**Credentials and Experience:**

- Bachelor's Degree or relevant industry experience
- CISSP
- PCI training or QSA completed/experience.
