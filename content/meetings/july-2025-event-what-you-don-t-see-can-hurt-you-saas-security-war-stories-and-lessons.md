**What You Don’t See Can Hurt You: A CISO and CEO Unpack SaaS Security Issues and share real-world war stories that can blindside any organization.**

Join us for an exclusive fireside chat featuring Jimmy Sanders, President of ISSA International and former CISO at Netflix, alongside Kevin Hanes, CEO at Reveal Security. Gain critical insights from these seasoned security leaders as they pull back the curtain on SaaS security risks, explore real-world incidents, and share actionable lessons learned from the trenches of cybersecurity leadership.

Whether you're responsible for securing your organization’s SaaS stack or looking to strengthen your understanding of evolving threats, this session promises to deliver hard-earned knowledge you won't find in any textbook.

Key Topics Covered:

- Hidden SaaS security vulnerabilities that can blindside organizations
- War stories from real-world SaaS incidents
- How SaaS and cloud complexity amplify cyber risks
- Strategies for improving SaaS visibility and detection
- Lessons learned from global cybersecurity leadership
- The evolving role of CISOs and CEOs in SaaS security

Why Attend:

- Learn directly from two globally recognized cybersecurity leaders.
- Hear real-world security stories that rarely make the headlines.
- Walk away with practical SaaS security strategies and frameworks.
- Network with top security professionals and industry peers.
- Stay ahead of emerging SaaS security trends and threats.
