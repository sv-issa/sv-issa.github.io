We are actively seeking an experienced Information Security Technician (McAfee Specialist) to join a rapidly growing startup. Well-funded and poised to double in size, this company's platform will transform how businesses interact.

**About the Position**

Our client is looking for an Information Security Technician with strong McAfee experience. This person will be a key contributor to the enterprise of security team and be responsible for protection of the corporate infrastructure.

- Help run the McAfee infrastructure
- Drive ePO to new levels of rigor and scalability
- McAfee healthy and operating within specific tolerances
- Help determine the path of our McAfee environment
- Retrieve and format reports for review by analysts and executives
- Review events that are detrimental to the overall security posture; triage alerts and escalate to analysts as needed
- Respond to alerts and perform basic incident response using a variety of security tools
- Perform correlation of events from a suite of security products
- Perform routine checklist tests to verify functionality and health of ePO
- Escalate issues to other engineers and work with vendor to resolve issues
- Interface with our internal customers to assist with their service requests
- This is a 24x7 security operations center

**Qualifications**

- Associates' degree.
- 2 years of experience in managing McAfee ePO and its clients
- 2-4 years of experience in IT - networking, system admin, programmer, or other advanced IT! You won't be successful here if you don't know how to do at least one of the following (preferably 2): Setup your own Windows domain; Create a web application from scratch; Set up an enterprise router; Use open source tools to build your own security platform; Setup a Gentoo system (or LFS, or any other Linux distro that isn't a next, next, finished install, or live DVD)
- Ability to troubleshoot potential issues on the network relating to availability issues from security devices
- Basic understanding of security systems and their role within an enterprise
- Ability to read and understand IP network designs and security fundamentals, including FW ACL's, Router configurations, and system alerts
- Experience working in a security operations center, network event analysis, threat analysis, or intelligence analysis is a plus
- Experience in IT ticketing triage and IT customer support

Experience in the following areas are a plus:
- Functional knowledge of and administrative experience on, Windows, Linux, MAC OS or UNIX Platforms
- Experience writing scripts to automate processes (BASH, Python, Perl, etc.) a plus
- Industry recognized professional certification (e.g., CISSP, CMSX, CMSA) a plus
- Knowledge of technology control initiatives such as ISO, SSAE 16, PCI, ITIL, and COBIT a plus
