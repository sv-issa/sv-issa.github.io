Join us for an unforgettable evening as ISSA Silicon Valley presents a rare fireside chat between Marnie Wilking, Chief Security Officer at [Booking.com](http://Booking.com), and Supervisory Special Agent Scott Hellman of the FBI. Get an insider’s perspective on the real-world battles cybersecurity leaders face—and how collaboration with law enforcement can tip the scales in your favor. This session will highlight how global enterprises structure their defenses, the technologies that work, and the people strategies that make it all possible.
