As 2025 unfolds, the cybersecurity landscape is being redefined by new technological breakthroughs in **Generative AI (Gen AI), agentic AI applications, and Quantum computing**. These advancements introduce **new risks, regulatory discussions, and evolving cyber threats**, making it easier than ever for attackers to develop sophisticated malware and execute social engineering attacks. **While adversaries are rapidly adopting AI-powered attack strategies, many companies are still relying on outdated security solutions.**

In this exclusive event, we’ll explore the **critical trends and challenges shaping the future of cybersecurity** and why **businesses of all sizes must pay attention**. We’ll also discuss the necessity of **edge-based security architectures** to counter evolving threats.

**Key Topics Covered**

- **Data Security Challenges** with Gen AI and agentic AI applications
- **Quantum computing risks** to existing network security solutions
- **Growing third-party and supply chain security threats**
- **Upcoming data privacy and AI-specific regulations**
- **Evolving edge-based security architectures** for modern threats

**Why Attend?**

- Gain **insights into cutting-edge threats** and how they’re reshaping security landscapes
- Learn from an **industry-leading cybersecurity expert** on **proactive security strategies**
- Understand how businesses can **adapt to emerging AI and quantum-driven threats**
- Network with **cybersecurity professionals, executives, and thought leaders**
- Enjoy **refreshments and engaging discussions** in an interactive setting
