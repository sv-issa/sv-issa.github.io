The following positions are up for re-election:

* Vice President
* Director of Membership
* Director of Communications
* Treasurer

**Key Dates**

* January 15 - Establish nominating committee and solicit nominations
* February 19 - Nomination Committee announces nominations
* February 28 - Ballots are distributed
* March 20 - Election is closed
* No meeting in March, email announcements only.
* March 26 - Results are announced

To run for office, you must be:
* A member in good standing with ISSA International
* A member of the Silicon Valley Chapter

To nominate someone you also must also be a member in good standing with ISSA International and a member of the Silicon Valley Chapter. Self-nominations are allowed.

To enter a nomination for the Silicon Valley Chapter Board, please send:
* A bio of nominee
* Description of goals
* A picture to this email alias: [elections@sv-issa.org](mailto:elections@sv-issa.org)

Emails to this location will reach our independent elections committee.
