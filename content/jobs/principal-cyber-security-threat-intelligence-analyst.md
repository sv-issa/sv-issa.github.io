Cyber security is an integral part of SVB's culture, and as a growing financial institution, it's vital that we all play our part in keeping customers' data secure. We're looking for a passionate, knowledgeable, and experienced Threat Intelligence Analyst. As a member of the Threat Intelligence Team within Cyber Security Operations you will advance the maturity of the threat intelligence program, provide intelligence for early threat detection, and provide intelligence to improve defenses.

**Responsibilities:**

- Continually work to improve quality and maturity of the threat intelligence program.
- Define and maintain standards and processes to meet the goals of the program.
- Monitor and interpret program improvement opportunities and recommend possible actions.
- Lead program improvement projects with responsibility for successful project execution.
- Select, assess, and operate threat intelligence tools.
- Work closely with other Cyber security teams to continually improve cyber defenses. Use influence and negotiation to achieve objectives.
- Analyze cyber threat and vulnerability information to produce timely, accurate and relevant threat intelligence. Provide advice on threats and alternatives to address them.
- Deliver tactical, operational, and strategic threat intelligence to team members at all levels with ad hoc communication, written reports, and presentations.
- Fulfill requests for information using external services and available resources.
- Establish and maintain relationships with internal and external partners to collect and share intelligence.
- Assess threat intelligence fosters on a periodic basis.
- Provide prioritization of threat hunt activities based on current threats.
