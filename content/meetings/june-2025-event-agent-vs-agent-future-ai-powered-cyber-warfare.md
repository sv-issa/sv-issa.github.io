In the past few years, venture capitalists have poured hundreds of millions of dollars into startups developing “cybersecurity digital employees.” These AI-powered agents are engineered to replace or augment human roles in security operations—from SOC analysts to incident responders. Meanwhile, threat actors are deploying their own offensive AI agents capable of persistent and autonomous attacks.

What happens when the battlefield is AI vs AI?
This talk explores the coming age of autonomous cyber warfare—how both sides will weaponize intelligent agents, and what it means for the future of security operations.

**Key Topics Covered**

- Rise of AI-powered cybersecurity agents
- Offensive vs defensive automation in cyber warfare
- How autonomous agents will change the role of the human analyst
- Ethical, strategic, and operational implications of AI on the front lines
- Emerging threats from adversarial use of AI in persistent attacks
